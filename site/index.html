What we do

One team from quay to doorstep

Most delays happen in the handover between the clearing agent, the warehouse and the transporter. We do all three, so your cargo has one file and one point of contact.

Customs clearing

We prepare and submit your customs declaration to SARS, work out the duties and VAT up front, and stay on the file until the release comes through.

  • Import and export declarations
  • Tariff classification and duty estimates before you ship
  • Handling customs stops and inspections for you
Words you will hear

Freight forwarding

We plan the whole move: booking space with the carrier, lining up the documents and keeping you posted from port of loading to final delivery.

  • Full container (FCL) and shared container (LCL) shipments
  • Imports and exports through South African ports
  • Document checks so nothing holds up the cargo
Words you will hear

Warehousing

Somewhere safe for your cargo between the port and your customer. We unpack containers, check the goods against the packing list and store them until you call them off.

  • Container unpacking and loading
  • Short and long term storage
  • Picking, palletising and dispatch
